Sweep — Price breaks a swing high or low, triggering retail stop losses and trapping breakout traders.Harga menembus swing high/low, memicu stop loss retail dan menjebak breakout trader.

Reversal — Price returns inside the range within the configured delay window, confirming the breakout was a liquidity hunt rather than a continuation.Harga berbalik masuk ke dalam range dalam batas delay yang ditentukan, mengkonfirmasi bahwa breakout tersebut adalah liquidity hunt.

MSS (Market Structure Shift) — The opposing internal structure must break, confirming a directional change in the market.Struktur internal berlawanan arah harus tembus, mengkonfirmasi pergantian arah market.

FVG / iFVG — The Fair Value Gap formed during displacement becomes the limit entry zone. An inverted FVG that failed to hold is used as a retest delivery zone.Fair Value Gap yang terbentuk saat displacement menjadi zona entry limit. Inversion FVG yang gagal hold digunakan sebagai zona retest delivery.

ERL — External Range Liquidity
Major swing highs/lows with a longer lookback. Represents large structural levels — typically deeper and slower to sweep. Swing high/low mayor dengan lookback panjang. Mewakili level struktural besar yang biasanya lebih dalam dan lebih lambat di-sweep.
IRL — Internal Range Liquidity
Minor swing highs/lows with a shorter lookback. Intraday levels that are touched more frequently and swept faster. Swing high/low minor dengan lookback pendek. Level intraday yang lebih sering disentuh dan lebih cepat di-sweep.

FVGs and iFVGs from a higher timeframe, used as a contextual bias filter. Can be set to Optional, Filter, or Required for ERL/IRL setup validation. FVG dan iFVG dari timeframe lebih tinggi, digunakan sebagai filter bias kontekstual. Bisa diset ke Optional, Filter, atau Required untuk validasi setup ERL/IRL.

HTF Delivery — Mode & After Sweep
Higher timeframe bias filter settings Pengaturan filter bias dari timeframe lebih tinggi
HTF ▾

HTF Delivery processes FVGs from a higher timeframe and uses them as quality filters for ERL/IRL setups. Turning off the HTF Delivery visual toggle only hides the zones — the filter logic remains active. HTF Delivery memproses FVG dari timeframe lebih tinggi dan menggunakannya sebagai filter kualitas untuk setup ERL/IRL. Mematikan toggle visual HTF Delivery hanya menyembunyikan zona — logika filter tetap aktif.

Optional
HTF has no effect on setup validation. All sweeps are accepted regardless of HTF zone presence. HTF tidak mempengaruhi validasi setup. Semua sweep diterima tanpa syarat zona HTF.
Filter
Setup is only valid if the sweep area overlaps any active HTF zone, regardless of direction. Setup hanya valid jika area sweep beririsan dengan zona HTF aktif manapun, tanpa memperhatikan arah.
Required
Setup must overlap an HTF zone with aligned direction. BSL sweep requires a bearish HTF zone; SSL sweep requires a bullish HTF zone. Setup harus beririsan dengan zona HTF yang searah. BSL sweep butuh zona HTF bearish; SSL sweep butuh zona HTF bullish.
After Sweep
OFF (Strict): HTF validation runs at sweep confirmation using the sweep range. ON (Forgiving): Validation is deferred to MSS or expiry, using the full expanded event range. OFF (Strict): Validasi HTF dilakukan saat sweep terkonfirmasi menggunakan range sweep. ON (Forgiving): Validasi ditunda sampai MSS atau expiry, menggunakan full event range yang sudah berkembang.
Strict produces fewer high-quality signals. Forgiving captures setups where HTF confluence forms after the initial sweep — common in instruments with post-sweep expansion like XAU/USD. Strict menghasilkan lebih sedikit sinyal berkualitas tinggi. Forgiving menangkap setup di mana HTF confluence baru terbentuk setelah sweep awal — umum terjadi di instrumen dengan post-sweep expansion seperti XAU/USD.

Pool, ATR & Tolerance
Minimum equal-level touches required before a level qualifies as a sweep target Minimum sentuhan equal level sebelum level dianggap valid sebagai sweep target
NEW ▾

Pool is the minimum number of times a level must be respected — touched and rejected — before it qualifies as a valid liquidity pool sweep target. The more touches, the greater the stop order accumulation at that level, and the more powerful the eventual sweep. Pool adalah jumlah minimum berapa kali sebuah level harus direspect — disentuh dan reject — sebelum dianggap sebagai liquidity pool sweep target yang valid. Semakin banyak touch, semakin besar akumulasi stop order di level tersebut, dan semakin powerful sweepnya.

Pool = 1
Every pivot immediately qualifies as a sweep target. Behavior is identical to the system without pool filtering — default. Setiap pivot langsung menjadi sweep target. Behavior identik dengan sistem tanpa pool filtering — default.
Pool = 2
Level must be respected at least twice (EQH/EQL) before a sweep counts as valid. Fewer signals, meaningfully higher quality. Level harus direspect minimal 2 kali (EQH/EQL) sebelum sweep dihitung valid. Lebih sedikit sinyal, kualitas lebih tinggi.
Pool = 3+
Highly selective. Triple EQH/EQL is one of the highest-probability setups due to the largest accumulated stop cluster. Sangat selektif. Triple EQH/EQL adalah salah satu setup dengan probabilitas tertinggi karena akumulasi stop cluster yang paling besar.
ATR Tolerance
Maximum price distance between two pivots to be considered "equal," expressed as an ATR multiplier. 0.15 = tight (near-identical levels only). 0.30 = moderate (allows minor variation). Jarak harga maksimum antara dua pivot agar dianggap "equal," dinyatakan sebagai multiplier ATR. 0.15 = ketat (hanya level yang hampir identik). 0.30 = moderat (toleransi variasi kecil).
Pool Visual:Visual Pool: When a sweep confirms from a pool with 2 or more touches, small markers appear at the wick of each intermediate pivot. The more markers, the stronger the accumulated pool that was swept. Ketika sweep terkonfirmasi dari pool dengan 2 atau lebih touch, marker kecil muncul tepat di wick setiap pivot intermediate. Semakin banyak marker, semakin kuat pool yang di-sweep.
Sweep Length & MSS Length
Pivot detection sensitivity for sweep levels and MSS reference levels Sensitivitas deteksi pivot untuk level sweep dan referensi MSS
▾

Sweep Length — How far back the system looks for swing highs/lows as liquidity levels. Higher values detect deeper, more structural levels that sweep less frequently but carry more weight. Lower values detect more frequent intraday levels. Seberapa jauh ke belakang sistem mencari swing high/low sebagai level liquidity. Nilai lebih besar mendeteksi level yang lebih dalam dan struktural yang jarang di-sweep tapi lebih berbobot. Nilai lebih kecil mendeteksi level intraday yang lebih sering.

MSS Length — Controls the sensitivity of the internal structure level that must break to confirm a market structure shift. Does not need to match Sweep Length. Mengontrol sensitivitas level struktur internal yang harus tembus untuk mengkonfirmasi market structure shift. Tidak perlu sama dengan Sweep Length.

Method, Sweep Break & MSS Break
How pivots are detected and how levels are considered broken Cara deteksi pivot dan cara level dianggap tembus
▾
Method: Pivot High/Low
Pivots confirmed symmetrically. More accurate but introduces a delay of 2× length bars before a level appears. Recommended for backtesting. Pivot dikonfirmasi secara simetris. Lebih akurat tapi ada delay 2× length bar sebelum level muncul. Direkomendasikan untuk backtesting.
Method: High/Lowest
Rolling extreme over the last N bars. Faster response with minor repaint risk when levels shift. Recommended for live trading. Rolling extreme dari N bar terakhir. Respons lebih cepat dengan risiko minor repaint saat level bergeser. Direkomendasikan untuk live trading.
Break: Close
Bar must close beyond the level. More conservative — filters false breaks from wick spikes. Canonical ICT method for MSS. Bar harus close melewati level. Lebih konservatif — menyaring false break dari spike wick. Metode ICT canonical untuk MSS.
Break: H/L
Wick touching the level counts as a break. More aggressive — catches wick-only sweeps common in algorithmic markets. Wick menyentuh level sudah dihitung sebagai break. Lebih agresif — menangkap wick-only sweep yang umum di pasar algoritmik.
Sweep Delay & MSS Delay
Time windows for reversal confirmation and MSS confirmation Window waktu untuk konfirmasi reversal dan konfirmasi MSS
▾

Sweep Delay — Number of bars price has to reverse after breaking the sweep level. If price does not reverse within this window, the sweep is discarded entirely. Jumlah bar yang diberikan untuk harga berbalik setelah menembus level sweep. Jika harga tidak berbalik dalam window ini, sweep dibatalkan sepenuhnya.

MSS Delay — Number of bars MSS has to confirm after the sweep reversal. When MSS Required is on and MSS does not arrive in time, the entire event is deleted from the chart. Jumlah bar yang diberikan untuk MSS terkonfirmasi setelah reversal sweep. Ketika MSS Required aktif dan MSS tidak datang tepat waktu, seluruh event dihapus dari chart.

XAU/USD M15: Sweep Delay 3 bars = 45 minutes. MSS Delay 10 bars = 2.5 hours. This is a reasonable starting range for intraday gold setups. Sweep Delay 3 bar = 45 menit. MSS Delay 10 bar = 2.5 jam. Ini adalah range awal yang reasonable untuk setup intraday gold.

▾
Optional
All FVGs displayed. No effect on signal validation. Semua FVG ditampilkan. Tidak ada efek pada validasi signal.
Filter
Only FVGs with direction opposite to the sweep are displayed. Signal validation is unchanged. Hanya FVG dengan arah berlawanan dari sweep yang ditampilkan. Validasi signal tidak berubah.

Required (iFVG)

After Sweep — ON: FVG detection window opens at sweep confirmation (only post-sweep FVGs count). OFF: window opens at the pivot bar (pre-sweep FVGs also count). Affects both display and signal validation. ON: Window deteksi FVG dibuka saat konfirmasi sweep (hanya FVG post-sweep yang dihitung). OFF: window dibuka di bar pivot (FVG pre-sweep juga dihitung). Mempengaruhi tampilan dan validasi signal.
MSS Required & Accept Cross MSS
Sweep lifecycle policy and cross-layer MSS acceptance Kebijakan lifecycle sweep dan penerimaan MSS lintas layer
▾

MSS Required — ON: A sweep with no MSS confirmation within the delay window is deleted entirely — no leftover visuals. OFF: The sweep persists on the chart even without MSS, showing all liquidity raids including those that continue without reversal. ON: Sweep yang tidak mendapat konfirmasi MSS dalam window delay dihapus sepenuhnya — tidak ada sisa visual. OFF: Sweep tetap di chart meskipun tidak ada MSS, menampilkan semua liquidity raid termasuk yang berlanjut tanpa reversal.

Accept Internal/External MSS — Allows structure shifts from the opposite layer to confirm sweeps. Enabling this on ERL allows an IRL MSS to confirm an ERL sweep — catching early reversals where lower timeframe structure breaks first. Mengizinkan structure shift dari layer lain untuk mengkonfirmasi sweep. Mengaktifkan ini di ERL memungkinkan IRL MSS untuk mengkonfirmasi ERL sweep — menangkap reversal awal di mana struktur timeframe lebih rendah tembus lebih dulu.

Entry Model & Signals Entry Model & Sinyal
Signal Model & Order TypeModel Signal & Order Type
Which event triggers the signal, and how the entry is placedEvent mana yang memicu sinyal dan bagaimana entry ditempatkan
▾
Sweep — Market / Limit
Signal at sweep + reversal confirmation. Market: entry at reversal bar close. Limit: entry when price returns to the swept level. Sinyal saat sweep + konfirmasi reversal. Market: entry di close bar reversal. Limit: entry saat harga kembali ke level swept.
MSS — Market Only
Signal at MSS confirmation. Entry at the MSS bar close. Later entry but with higher structural confirmation. Sinyal saat konfirmasi MSS. Entry di close bar MSS. Entry lebih late tapi dengan konfirmasi struktural lebih tinggi.
FVG — Limit Only
Entry when price retraces into a valid FVG. Requires an active MSS. Fill Ratio sets the required retrace depth into the zone. Entry saat harga retrace ke FVG yang valid. Membutuhkan MSS aktif. Fill Ratio menentukan kedalaman retrace yang diperlukan ke dalam zona.
iFVG — Market / Limit
Market: entry at the moment an FVG flips to iFVG. Limit: entry when price retraces into the iFVG zone after inversion. Market: entry saat FVG flip menjadi iFVG. Limit: entry saat harga retrace ke zona iFVG setelah inversion.
HTF FVG — Limit Only
Entry when price retraces into an active HTF FVG zone. Operates independently of ERL/IRL scope. Entry saat harga retrace ke zona HTF FVG yang aktif. Beroperasi independen dari scope ERL/IRL.
HTF iFVG — Market / Limit
Entry at an HTF FVG inversion or its retest. The most selective model — rarest setups, highest structural quality. Entry di inversion HTF FVG atau retestnya. Model paling selektif — setup paling jarang, kualitas struktural tertinggi.
Fill Ratio — Controls the required retrace depth for Limit orders. 0.01 = zone edge (aggressive early entry). 0.50 = consequent encroachment / midline (canonical ICT reference). 1.00 = far edge (conservative deep pullback). Ignored by Market orders. Mengontrol kedalaman retrace yang diperlukan untuk Limit order. 0.01 = edge zona (agresif, entry awal). 0.50 = consequent encroachment / midline (referensi ICT canonical). 1.00 = far edge (konservatif, pullback dalam). Diabaikan oleh Market order.

Risk Management Risk Management
SL Method
5 stop loss placement methods with automatic fallback7 metode penempatan stop loss dengan fallback otomatis
▾
Percentage
Fixed distance from entry as a % of price. Simple and uniform across all models. ATR buffer is ignored.Jarak tetap dari entry sebagai % harga. Sederhana dan konsisten di semua model. ATR buffer diabaikan.
Auto
Structure-aware placement. Sweep → beyond peak/trough. MSS → beyond event range extreme. FVG/iFVG/HTF → beyond zone boundary.Penempatan berbasis struktur. Sweep → di luar peak/trough. MSS → di luar range extreme event. FVG/iFVG/HTF → di luar batas zona.
Sweep Level
SL beyond the swept liquidity level. Tightest structural option. Higher stop-out probability on noise.SL di luar level liquidity yang di-sweep. Opsi struktural paling ketat. Risiko stop-out lebih tinggi pada noise.
Sweep Peak/Trough
SL beyond the wick extreme of the sweep. Safest structural option. Lower R:R per trade.SL di luar wick ekstrem dari sweep. Opsi struktural paling aman. R:R lebih rendah per trade.
Event Range Extreme
SL beyond the entire event range from pivot to MSS. Widest option. Significantly lower R:R.SL di luar seluruh range event dari pivot ke MSS. Opsi paling lebar. R:R jauh lebih rendah.
FVG / iFVG / HTF Zone
SL beyond the boundary of the zone that triggered entry. Falls back to Auto if zone data is unavailable at trigger time.SL di luar batas zona yang memicu entry. Fallback ke Auto jika data zona tidak tersedia saat trigger.
ATR Buffer — Adds extra distance (ATR × multiplier) beyond the structural SL reference. Useful for volatile instruments that frequently spike beyond structure before reversing. Has no effect when using the Percentage method. Menambahkan jarak ekstra (ATR × multiplier) di luar referensi SL struktural. Berguna untuk instrumen volatile yang sering spike melewati struktur sebelum berbalik. Tidak berpengaruh saat menggunakan metode Percentage.
Risk % & R:R
Automatic position sizing based on initial capital and SL distanceSizing posisi otomatis berdasarkan modal awal dan jarak SL
▾

Risk — Percentage of initial capital risked per trade. Position size is calculated automatically: Initial Capital × Risk% ÷ SL Distance. Always references the initial capital — not running equity — so backtest results remain consistent and comparable across periods. Persentase modal awal yang dirisk per trade. Position size dihitung otomatis: Modal Awal × Risk% ÷ Jarak SL. Selalu mengacu ke modal awal — bukan running equity — sehingga hasil backtest tetap konsisten dan comparable antar periode.

R:R — Take profit distance as a multiple of SL distance. R:R 3.0 places TP at exactly 3× the SL distance from entry price. Jarak take profit sebagai kelipatan dari jarak SL. R:R 3.0 menempatkan TP tepat di 3× jarak SL dari harga entry.
