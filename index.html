What is This Strategy

Liquidity Concept is a complete price delivery model. It maps out how smart money builds up pools of liquidity at key swing points, triggers a sweep of that liquidity, then uses the displacement and fair value gap left behind to deliver price back in the intended direction.

The framework is not based on indicators or oscillators. It is a structural model — it reads price through the lens of who is taking and providing liquidity at each stage of the market cycle.

Analysis Layers

The strategy works across three distinct structural layers. Each operates independently with its own detection parameters, but they interact during confirmation and filter stages.

ERL

External Range Liquidity

Tracks major swing highs and lows. These are the primary structural levels where institutional liquidity pools tend to form. Sweeps at ERL carry higher structural significance and typically precede larger displacement moves.

IRL

Internal Range Liquidity

Tracks minor intraday swing points within the broader ERL range. Useful for fine-tuning entry timing and identifying smaller delivery sequences nested inside the larger move.

HTF

Higher Timeframe Delivery

Tracks fair value gaps from a higher timeframe directly on the current chart. HTF zones act as a directional bias context — entries on ERL and IRL gain additional confluence when price is approaching or inside an active HTF zone.

Event must overlap any active HTF zone — direction of the zone does not matter. Pure presence check.

Required

Event must overlap an HTF zone with directional alignment. Bull sweep requires a bearish HTF zone; sell sweep requires a bullish HTF zone. Inversion zones aligned with sweep direction also qualify.

After Sweep
Toggle ▼
Controls when the HTF overlap check is evaluated. Both modes reflect valid interpretations of the liquidity model — the choice trades off speed for coverage.
Off — Strict

HTF check runs at sweep confirmation using only the sweep range. If no HTF zone is present at that moment, the setup is rejected immediately. Produces fewer, higher-quality signals.

On — Deferred

HTF check is deferred to MSS confirmation or event expiry. The full event range is used for the overlap check, capturing setups where price expands into an HTF zone after the sweep. More signals, slightly lower precision.

Off

All HTF zones are tracked regardless of their size. May include very small gaps or oversized news spikes.

Internal Range Liquidity (IRL)

IRL settings are structurally identical to ERL settings. The key difference is that IRL uses shorter default lookback lengths to target minor intraday structure. All the same options apply — Pool, Detection Method, MSS Required, FVG Mode, iFVG Mode, After Sweep, and Accept External MSS.

Off

IRL events confirm only via their own layer structure. Faster, more frequent confirmations. Default behavior.

Glossary

L

Liquidity Pool

A cluster of pending orders sitting at or near a key price level — typically formed by equal highs or lows, stop losses from retail traders, or untested swing points.

S

Sweep

A move that briefly breaks a key level, triggers the orders sitting there, then reverses. The reversal is what confirms a true sweep versus a genuine breakout.

F

FVG

Fair Value Gap. A three-candle imbalance where the middle candle moves so strongly that it leaves a gap between the high of the first and the low of the third. Price tends to return to fill this gap.

i

iFVG

Inverse FVG. A fair value gap that has been fully closed through. Once a bullish FVG is completely traded through to the downside, it becomes a bearish iFVG — same price range, opposite delivery direction.

M

MSS

Market Structure Shift. A confirmed break of a prior swing point in the opposite direction to the sweep. Required to confirm that a reversal is underway and the sweep was genuine.

CE

Consequent Encroachment

The 50% midpoint of any FVG or iFVG zone. The standard reference for limit order placement — price reaching the midpoint is considered sufficient retrace into the zone for a high-probability entry.
